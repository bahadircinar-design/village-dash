Village Dash

An e-bike chase to bring a lost pup home

A happy little pup slipped its leash and is zooming through the sunny village. Hop on your electric dirt bike, follow it through the alleys and catch it gently: match its speed up close, corner it in a dead end, or find where it hides.
WASD / arrows ride · Space drift (handbrake) · R reset · Esc pause · M mute · gamepad works too
Left pad: steer. Right: GO throttle, BRAKE, DRIFT for sharp turns.
Original game. All art and music are generated in code. No pups are harmed: the bike brakes for you near the dog.
